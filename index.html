Content Operations

Luna Forge

One creator's AI-assisted workspace for short-form video

Luna Forge researches trending angles, drafts scripts and captions, and publishes finished short-form videos to its owner's own TikTok account — a personal content-operations tool, not a public platform.

What it does

RESEARCH
Scans trending content angles and scores them for relevance before anything is made — keeps output grounded in what's actually working, not guesswork.
DRAFT
Writes hooks, beat-by-beat scripts, on-screen text and captions for short-form video, ready for review.
PUBLISH
Uploads finished videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts via the Content Posting API — a human reviews and taps Post themselves.
TRACK
Reads basic performance data (views, engagement) on the owner's own published content to inform what to make next.
Scope: Luna Forge is operated by and for a single owner, on their own accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public, does not create accounts for other people, and does not access or store data belonging to any other TikTok user.

Why "Luna Forge"

The name pairs two ideas: Luna — the quiet, research-first phase where content ideas are gathered and shaped — and Forge — where those ideas get made into something real and published. It's a small, deliberately single-purpose tool: it doesn't run itself unsupervised, and every public post starts as a draft a human reviews.
