Content operations

Luna Forge

A personal content-operations tool for researching, drafting, and publishing social content on one owner's own accounts.

What it does

  • Researches trending content angles and scores them before any content is made.
  • Drafts scripts, hooks, and captions for short-form video.
  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts, for the owner to review and post themselves.
  • Tracks basic performance (views, engagement) on the owner's own published content.
Scope: Luna Forge is operated by and for a single owner, on their own accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public, does not create accounts for other people, and does not access or store data belonging to any other TikTok user.
